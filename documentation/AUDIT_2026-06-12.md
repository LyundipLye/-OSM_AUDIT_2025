## A. Fixes applied

### A1. Construction-date inconsistency (code bug) — FIXED
`scripts/11_plot_evapotranspiration.py` ran its statistical regime-shift test at `CONSTRUCTION_DATE = 2021-06-01` (consistent with every other script) but **hardcoded 2019** in the chart: the BACI marker was labelled "Construction Start (Jun 2019)" and the annual bar chart highlighted the 2019 bar as the construction year. The figure therefore contradicted its own statistics.
**Fix:** both the annotation and the bar-highlight year now derive from `CONSTRUCTION_DATE`, so the chart and the test agree. Label changed from "Construction Start" to "BACI Split" (more accurate for a phased conversion).

### A2. BACI threshold was never justified in prose — FIXED
The `2021-06-01` split is the single most consequential analytical choice and appeared nowhere in the README as a justified decision.
**Fix:** added a "BACI threshold" paragraph to §2 stating that the split is the mid-point of the documented 2020–2023 conversion window, that a mid-window split biases the estimated shift *downward* (conservative), and that the transect uses clean pre/post windows to avoid the transition years. Met Office wording aligned to the same 2021 split.

### A3. Inverted LST input-file naming — CLARIFIED (files not renamed)
The **primary** full-polygon analysis (`07`) reads `ee-chart_lst_sensitivity.csv`; the **sensitivity** parking-lot check (`07b`) reads `ee-chart_lst.csv`. The filenames are the reverse of their roles — a reviewer would assume the file called "sensitivity" holds the sensitivity check.
**Fix:** files left as-is (renaming breaks provenance with the archived GEE exports and the scripts), but an explicit "LST input-file map" table was added to README §2.B, and header notes were added to `07` and `07b` stating the mapping and warning not to swap inputs.

### A4. NDVI — absolute vs DiD figures conflated — FIXED
§3 reported the raw Impact-zone decline (0.635 → 0.28) and the DiD HAC shift (−0.36) as if interchangeable.
**Fix:** §3 now separates (i) the absolute Impact-zone fall from (ii) the DiD regime shift actually tested in `05`, and explains that the two nearly coincide *only* because the Control Zone is stable. The near-zero HAC p-value is reframed as evidence for the direction/persistence of the shift, not as precision on the point estimate.

### A5. Overclaiming on non-significant LST results — FIXED
§3 described the parking-lot annual composite as "statistically and practically relevant" at **Welch p = 0.071 (n.s.)**, resting on ~10 annual means.
**Fix:** the composite is now reported as a *suggestive* practical-magnitude signal that does not reach significance and carries wide uncertainty (low power, unstable Cohen's d at n≈6/4). The defensible thermodynamic claim is relocated to the *convergence across pipelines*, not this single underpowered test.

### A6. Met Office baseline comparison overstated — FIXED
§3 claimed the +2.03 °C composite "exceeds" the +0.37 °C regional baseline and is therefore "primarily driven by localised anthropogenic" heating.
**Fix:** reframed as *directional and indicative*, not a quantified attribution — noting that +2.03 °C is itself n.s., that LST (land-surface) and HadUK-Grid (screen-level air temperature) are different physical quantities, and that a formal test would require propagating both uncertainties.

### A7. Spatial transect — hidden contradiction surfaced as an honest negative — FIXED
The §2 framing implied the transect corroborated a warm distance-decay scar "consistent with sensible heat advection". The archived `ee-chart_decay.csv` shows the opposite: every ring is cooler post-construction, and the **background-subtracted core net anomaly is ≈ −1.5 °C** — a daytime *cool* core (high-albedo asphalt under a ~10:30 overpass).
**Fix:** §2.C "Physical Interpretation" rewritten to report this as a deliberate negative result that bounds the method — a warm advective scar is a *nocturnal* phenomenon undetectable by daytime-only Landsat (consistent with Limitation 8), resolvable only with night-pass sensors. This converts a latent reviewer objection into a strength.

### A8. Self-contradictory description of `07b` — FIXED (description) / FLAGGED (numbers)
§2.C simultaneously said `07b` "isolates the ~3-pixel parking lot" *and* "expands to the full 1 km² ~50-pixel polygon" — the second describes `07`, not `07b`.
**Fix:** the `07b` block now describes only what `07b` does (narrow the ROI to the parking-lot core, vary the temporal window), with the power trade-off stated explicitly. The numeric discrepancy is flagged below (B1).

### B1b. Note on the daytime warming vs the transect cool core
The parking-lot BACI shows daytime warming (+1.08 °C) while the transect (A7) shows a daytime *cool* core. These are not contradictory: BACI compares the same pixels' Impact−Control delta through time (temporal contrast), whereas the transect compares the core against its own far-field within single daytime composites (spatial contrast). Both are daytime; the BACI delta captures the loss of the control's relative cooling, the transect captures the asphalt's high daytime albedo relative to surrounding vegetation. Worth one sentence in the discussion so a reader doesn't read them as conflicting.

### 🔴 D1. "+5 °C Net Increase on LST" (EO results slide) — WRONG, must replace
The slide shows an **uncontrolled single-zone linear trend** (LST scatter 2015–2026 with a straight OLS line) annotated "+5 °C". This is the naive method the repo explicitly replaced. The verified, controlled BACI results are:
- Primary full-polygon: **+0.56 °C** (HAC p = 0.061; MW p = 0.041)

### 🔴 D2. "365-day rolling mean smoothing" (ST & EO Pipeline slide) — WRONG, must remove
The slide advertises "365-day rolling mean smoothing … to expose structural ecological trends." Smoothing a series and *then* running trend/significance tests manufactures autocorrelation and inflates apparent significance — exactly why the repo abandoned it. README now states: "GEE exports raw per-overpass observations (no smoothing applied); all statistical testing is performed on raw observations (HAC OLS) or monthly-resampled means (Seasonal Mann-Kendall)." **Replace** the smoothing line with the actual method: raw observations → DiD vs control → Newey-West HAC OLS + Seasonal Mann-Kendall.

- Deck says **"USGS Landsat 8"** only; repo uses **Landsat 7 + 8 + 9 triple-satellite fusion**.

Keep the dramatic absolute collapse if desired, but label it as the Impact-zone series and add the DiD result (the quantity actually tested).
